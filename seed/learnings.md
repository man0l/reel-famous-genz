# reel-famous-genz: learnings

What worked and what didn't, newest last. Read this before writing a topic or script. Add lines with `rfg.sh learn "<text>"` (board feedback, view counts after 48 h, render problems).

## Carried over from the genz-face pack (reel-factory, SLA-1643, 21-22 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-22: The board rejected the boy-group / idol-position framing ("you don't listen"). Facial features only, the @glowupmenclub way.
- 2026-11-22: The board asked whether the hooks were the same as @glowupmenclub's. Reworded hooks don't count; use their exact lines.
- 2026-11-22: Their biggest reels are self-tests with no celebrity (hair damage 1.67M, stop shampoo 833k, attractive to girls 662k). The celebrity breakdowns do 50k-500k.
- 2026-11-21: A VPS render takes about 20 min (0.6 fps, 1230 frames) and dies if its parent run ends. Start it with `setsid` and a log file, and keep TMPDIR inside the data home.
- 2026-11-21: Cloudflare renders need the composition in the deployed reel-remotion image (deploy runs on a push to reel-factory main).
